# YALANOIS® Mockup Supply

A responsive, no-build product mockup showcase for YALANOIS. It runs locally in a browser and links visitors directly to the Creative Market and Gumroad profiles.

## Open it locally

1. Download or copy this folder to your laptop.
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.

The page works without installing anything. Google Fonts load when you have an internet connection; the layout and mockup artwork are included locally.

## Update it

- Edit product names, category labels, and marketplace destinations in `index.html`.
- Product cards use editable inline CSS artwork for the preview scenes. Replace the markup inside each `.product-image` with an `<img>` when your real product previews are ready.
- Change colors, typography, and mobile layout in `styles.css`.
- Category filters and the small-screen navigation are in `app.js`.

- Gumroad: https://yalanois.gumroad.com/
